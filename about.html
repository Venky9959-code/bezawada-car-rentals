OUR STORY & VISION

Born in the City of Victory, Built for Modern Travelers

Founded with a dedication to elevating travel across Andhra Pradesh, Bezawada Car Rentals emerged from a simple observation: travelers, families, and professionals needed a car rental partner that combined pristine vehicles with absolute transparency and unmatched punctuality.

Whether you are arriving at Gannavaram Airport for urgent business in the capital region of Amaravati, seeking blessing at the sacred Kanaka Durga Temple atop Indrakeeladri, or planning an adventurous coastal getaway to Suryalanka and Mypadu Beach, we put the steering wheel of premium mobility in your hands.

Today, with a growing fleet spanning practical hatchbacks like the Maruti Swift, rugged 4x4 SUVs like the Mahindra Thar, spacious family haulers like the Toyota Innova Crysta, and statement luxury sedans like the BMW 320d, we are proud to be Andhra's highest-rated self-drive brand.
